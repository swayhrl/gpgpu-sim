# Paper Reproduction Report Template

## Scope

State simulator version, GPU/config family, workload/input coverage, and whether results are exact, inferred, app-level, or unavailable.

## Results

Report only observed current-environment metrics. Do not claim paper absolute speedup/energy reproduction unless the original simulator, GPU, inputs, and phase mapping are matched.

## Limitations

List unavailable workloads, missing fields, correctness caveats, and next actions.
